01 · Invention

The sentence that must survive review

This is not a new climbing minigame and not a new renderer. It is the causal field specialized onto a near-vertical face, plus a body that is allowed to peel off.

Author a multiresolution rock field on a cliff. Project it into Gaussian crystals, flakes, seeps and lichen. Climb with four independent contact patches whose per-patch load writes the same field they read. If lithology, moisture and fracture do not change both the picture and the grip, it is a skin — delete it.

What already exists

  • Causal field primitive — R(state, env, direction)
  • Demo A snow / B trail / C fire — same compiler + evaluator
  • Trail overhaul: 4-wheel Rapier sampler, FeelSpec, fail conditions
  • ground-from-splat capability
  • Poseidon / Gaia / three-vfx / kimodo (editor-only)
  • FieryGS Apache-2.0 audit already on the books
  • Harness: SKILL.md + agent verbs, assemble-first

What this expansion adds

  • Cliff as a field face, not a collider slab with hold props
  • Carpenter statistical subdivision for macro / meso roughness
  • Joint-set geology: dip, dip-direction, spacing, persistence
  • Hold types inferred from curvature × edge × μ × reach
  • World-space 4-patch climber (2 hands, 2 feet) + COM
  • Dual contact law: smear friction vs positive edge
  • Weather writes: seep, freeze, polish, flake detach

04 · Ancestor

Carpenter 1980 is the right grandfather. It is not the hold solver.

Computer rendering of fractal curves and surfaces (SIGGRAPH 1980) introduced statistical subdivision: split a geometric entity while preserving statistical character. Vol Libre started from ~100 big triangles and recursively quartered them until the triangles disappeared. That is the macro and meso of our cliff. The causal field is what those triangles mean.

Steal

  • Statistical subdivision of a coarse face mesh
  • Preserve lithology character at every level (Hurst / roughness exponent per rock type)
  • On-the-fly detail as the climber approaches (10 km → 10 cm, same contract as Vol Libre)
  • Independently: Fournier / Fussell stochastic modeling, same conference

Do not steal

  • Uniform Brownian roughness as “the geology”
  • Infinite quartered triangles with no joint sets — that is noise, not rock
  • Mandelbrot as a hold generator (the user named the ancestor correctly; the field does the rest)
  • A second terrain engine beside the existing mountain world

05 · World

A cliff is a face of cells, not a wall mesh

Author the geology a body is allowed to suffer on. Difficulty is the distribution of support under four patches — not a YDS slider painted onto a collider.

Face schemaworld.cliff
CliffFace
  coarseMesh          # Carpenter root triangles / implicit block union
  lithology           # granite | sandstone | limestone | basalt | mixed
  joints[]            # {dip_deg, dip_dir_deg, spacing_m, persistence_m, aperture_mm}
  bedding?            # sandstone / limestone only
  tiles[]             # overlapping field tiles + splat tiles on the face
  seeps[]             # moisture wells (Poseidon streaks)
  freezeBand          # altitude / aspect / season
  capture?            # optional Gaussian capture of a real crag
  routes?             # discovered or hinted — never the physics path

The “route” is a comment the agent or a local can leave. The body never rides it. If the climber is constrained to a hold graph the overhaul failed.

Cell state (rock)

  • height / normal (from subdivision + capture)
  • curvature (mean + Gaussian) — pocket vs bulge
  • edge 0–1 — fracture intersection sharpness
  • lithology + grain
  • moisture 0–1 (seep / rain / melt)
  • ice 0–1 (Demo A leftover on the same face)
  • looseness — flake / block detach risk
  • polish — traffic-worn smear
  • temperature

Derived response

  • μ peak / μ smear for the contact law
  • positive-edge pull-off vs friction-only smear
  • hold class: jug · incut · crimp · sloper · pinch · pocket · smear
  • usable area for hand vs foot (different sample radii)
  • flake detach probability under impulse
  • audio grain (crystal tick, sandy hiss, wet slap)
  • SH / albedo / spec / covariance hints
06 · Inference

A hold is a query, not an object

At runtime the climber never looks up Hold#482. It asks the field: given this patch radius, this pull direction, this moisture — how much force can I take before I peel?

Inferred classField signatureFails when
Jug / incutNegative curvature pocket + high edge + dryFilled with ice or running seep
CrimpSharp positive edge, tiny usable areaEdge rounds (polish) or flake loosens
SloperLow curvature, high μ demand, large smear areaMoisture film, lichen, heat-soft skin
PinchTwo opposing faces within hand widthOne face is a detached flake
PocketDeep concavity, limited finger countSand fills it; ice lenses
Smear / slabAlmost no edge; μ × area × angle onlyAny drop in μ. The honest slab.
No-holdConvex bulge, low μ, no edgeAlways — this is the blank face
Debug overlay is allowed. A Climb-5-style hold graph can be visualized from the inference so designers and agents can see what the field thinks. That overlay is not serialized as the world. Regen the cliff, the overlay changes.

08 · Grammars

Granite, sandstone, limestone are different games

maxliebscher’s Three.js rock lab already refuses a generic displacement recipe. We steal that posture: form grammars, not texture swaps. Climbing character falls out.

Granite

Broad lobes, restrained joints, crystals, rounded weathering. Slopers and smears. Cracks where two joint families meet. Wet = glass.

Yosemite / Elbe sandstone-adjacent slabs in Saxony differ — do not collapse them

Sandstone

Persistent bedding, banks, undercuts, pockets. Holds are sculpted by bedding + wind. Wet sandstone can be a different rock.

Sächsische Schweiz is local truth for Dresden

Limestone

Block structure, dissolution pockets, continuous fracture distances. Tufas, pockets, sharp edges. Seeps live in the karst.

09 · Body

The climber is a four-patch sampler with a center of mass

UpRoom’s 2021 write-up is the locomotion ancestor: five effectors (L/R hand, L/R foot, root), LAST / CURRENT / NEXT, IK to targets. We keep the effector math. We throw away their pre-baked hold queue. Targets are field queries.

Contact lawdual regime
ContactSample
  position, normal
  mu_peak, mu_smear
  edge            # 0..1 positive interlocking
  area            # usable patch for this limb
  looseness
  moisture, ice, temperature

force_budget =
    edge * pull_off(lithology, aperture)          # "I can hang on this"
  + mu_smear * N * smear_area                     # "I can stand on this"
  - ice_penalty - seep_penalty - polish_penalty

peel if  sum(force_budget) < body_weight * load_share + dynamic

Smear-only contacts are legal — that is slab climbing. Edge-only contacts are legal — that is a tiny crimp. Zero-budget contacts are legal — that is a dead point that will not hold. The FeelSpec decides whether a peel is “player missed” or “field lied”.

Rapier job

Rigid torso + limb constraints. Contacts are sampled, not magnetized. A patch that loses budget becomes a sliding constraint, then a free body. Same spirit as a wheel that unloads on a shelf.

@3jse/physics-rapier

IK job

Place effectors on the best field query inside a reach cone. Spider-style surface-normal clinging (majidmanzarpour) is the wall-walk reference, not the human gait. Kimodo stays editor-only motion gen.

13 · Phases

Gates, not vibes

0 Face tile on the existing mountain

One Carpenter-subdivided face, one lithology, no climber. Gate: joint set is visible in the mesh and in the field debugger.

1 Inference overlay

Hold-class histogram from curvature × edge × μ. Climb-5-style debug dots. Gate: regenerating the seed changes the dots. No serialized hold list.

2 Four-patch dummy

A rigid body with four probes. Hang, smear, peel. Gate: dry sloper holds; field.addSeep peels it. Same evaluator as snow / trail.

3 Human IK + Rapier

UpRoom effector loop, spider normal blend, Reach cones query the field. Gate: a player can leave the trail, get on the face, and fall off honestly.

4 Weather writes

Seep, verglas (Demo A on Demo D), flake detach, polish from traffic. Gate: second pull on a loose flake is a different rock.

5 Mountain-sport loop

Approach trail (Demo B) → slab → vertical → optional roof. Agent verbs lay joints and seeps. Stop. Do not build a route setter.
